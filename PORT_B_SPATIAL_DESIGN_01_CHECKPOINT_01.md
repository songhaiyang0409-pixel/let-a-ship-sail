# CHECKPOINT 01 — CANDIDATE COMPARISON

Three isolated geography candidates were run with the same 648x1152 gameplay camera and deterministic traversal harness.

- A: offset narrow inlet; clear split entrance but more symmetric and marker-like at the working shore.
- B: dog-leg cove; best separation between exposed approach, constrained entrance, inner water, and a single working shoreline.
- C: asymmetric high headland; strong scale but the shoreline relationship was less direct.

Selected for continuation: B. No R03 files were changed.
