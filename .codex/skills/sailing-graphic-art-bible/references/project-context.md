# Project context

This bible is project-local and supplements `graphic-sailing-art-director`. The current approved product boundary is a quiet mobile sailing focus game: the player is the captain, the boat is the player's body in the world, and the core emotional arc is departure, open-water movement, destination approach, arrival, and remembered time.

Default sailing should remain close to zero traditional HUD. The boat must have a real cabin exterior even when no interior gameplay is implemented. Character species, crew composition, and story roles remain undecided and should not be treated as canonical production constraints. Land is currently a destination and spatial reference, not a freely explorable character space.

When a task is isolated, preserve the formal Sea Trial, Journey Test, Port-to-Port, production water, production controls, and `project.godot` unless the task explicitly authorizes a formal change. Do not infer missing parts of a future production-foundation mission.
