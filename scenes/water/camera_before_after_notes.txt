PORT-TO-PORT FIX PASS 01 - CAMERA DIAGNOSIS

Before
- The isolated slice stored raw mouse orbit values and applied them directly to the desired camera heading.
- Horizontal input used a different sensitivity from the approved Sea Trial camera.
- Vertical input changed camera height only; it did not rotate the camera offset and target around the boat pivot.
- Boat-follow smoothing and camera-look smoothing were separate from the approved behavior, so the same drag could feel different while the boat was moving.

After
- Copied the approved Sea Trial camera behavior into this isolated controller without changing the formal camera script.
- Mouse relative input is applied once, without frame-delta multiplication.
- Horizontal look uses 0.0045 radians per mouse pixel and wraps continuously.
- Vertical look uses 0.0030 radians per mouse pixel and is clamped to -0.17..0.35 radians.
- Look targets are smoothed at 7.0; camera follow is smoothed at 8.0; heading follow is smoothed at 1.65.
- Camera position and target rotate together around the boat pivot, preserving the approved drag semantics.
- R resets look targets to the default rear three-quarter camera and lets smoothing return the view.

Formal Sea Trial camera: unchanged.
