# BOAT WATER CLEAN BASELINE 03

## Result

- Removed the hull-side and bow stamped patch effects from the isolated RND 01 test.
- Preserved Prototype C's world-space fragmented stern wake, curved turn history,
  bounded lifetime, and one-way fade after stopping.
- Retained no hull-contact effect: an almost invisible contact cue is safer than
  another obviously artificial effect at this stage.

## Why

The previous side/bow quads had fixed rectangular boundaries and repeated pale
coverage. They read as footprints, scales, or sprites instead of displaced
water. The stern wake is generated by a separate historical sample path, so the
bad contact effect could be removed without changing wake architecture.

## Verification

The isolated scene was run with the project's Godot 4.7.2 executable:

- straight cruise and capture run completed;
- auto-demo completed through cruise, turn, stop, and restart;
- stop check reported speed=0.00 while stopped, then new wake samples after
  restart;
- no formal Sea Trial, Journey Test, Port-to-Port scene, island, boat model,
  project.godot, or formal wake code was changed.

- Bow-water displacement is intentionally absent rather than represented by a
  fake sprite effect.
- The normal gameplay camera shows a restrained, relatively small wake.
- Natural whitecaps and physical bow hydrodynamics remain future work.
- Final visual judgment still requires human playtest.
